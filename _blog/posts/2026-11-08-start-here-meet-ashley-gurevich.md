If you're new here, welcome! I have so many new faces lately, so I wanted to take a moment to introduce myself.

My name is Ashley Gurevich. I've been a loan officer for about five years, and before that I managed a bank for 10 years. I almost became a financial advisor (I had my Series 7 license and everything), but I found my passion helping people buy homes and I never looked back.

So what do I do? I help anyone from first-time home buyers to move-up buyers to investment buyers, and I have all the products under the sun. Here's how that looks for you.

## If you're a first-time buyer

We create a game plan for you. We start with a soft pull only application, so it doesn't run your credit score, and it gives us a clear picture of where you are today.

And if you're not there yet, that's okay! I walk you through it step by step until all the puzzle pieces are put together and you have the keys to your home.

## If you're ready to move up

Maybe you're looking for your next dream home and asking yourself, "Is it possible to buy my home without selling my home first?" It is! We dial down all the numbers, look at the pros and cons, and create a plan together. You can read a real client story about how this works in [How a Bridge Loan Got My Clients Into Their Dream Home Before They Sold](/blog/buy-before-you-sell-bridge-loan-client-story).

## If you're wondering whether to rent or buy

Trying to figure out if you should rent or buy? We'll look at your real numbers side by side so you can make the choice that fits your goals.

## If you're a business owner or 1099 employee

I have special products for business owners and 1099 employees that can help you get a loan without your tax returns. Learn more about [bank statement loans](/bank-statement-loans) and [1099 loans](/1099-loans).
