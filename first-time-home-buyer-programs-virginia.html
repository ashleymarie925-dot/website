First-Time Home Buyer Programs in Virginia

Virginia Housing (you may know it as VHDA) runs real grants - including one that never gets repaid. Here's the accurate 2026 list.

The Programs, Accurately

Down Payment Assistance Grant - a true grant (never repaid) covering a percentage of your purchase price, for first-time buyers within income limits, paired with a Virginia Housing first mortgage. This is the headline program - free money toward your down payment.

Plus Second Mortgage - for buyers over the grant's limits (or with different needs): a second mortgage that covers the down payment so you can close with minimal cash.

Closing Cost Assistance Grant - designed for VA and USDA loans: a grant toward closing costs that pairs beautifully with $0-down VA financing. If you're military in Hampton Roads or NoVa, see my Virginia VA loan page - this stack is the play.

About the MCC: Virginia Housing's Mortgage Credit Certificate (a federal tax-credit program) has been suspended since 2023. Plenty of websites still advertise it - it is not currently available, and anyone promising it isn't reading the current guidelines. If it ever returns, this page and my clients will know.

Questions

Straight Answers

Does the Virginia Housing grant really not get repaid?
Correct - the Down Payment Assistance Grant is a true grant with no repayment, unlike second-mortgage-style assistance. Income limits, first-time buyer rules, and pairing with a Virginia Housing first mortgage apply.
What income limits apply?
They vary by region and household size - Northern Virginia's limits differ from Hampton Roads'. Tell me your county and household size and I'll check same-day.
Is the Virginia MCC tax credit available?
No - it's been suspended since 2023 despite still appearing on many websites. The active options are the DPA Grant, Plus Second Mortgage, and Closing Cost Assistance Grant.
Can these stack with a VA loan?
The Closing Cost Assistance Grant is specifically built for VA and USDA loans - see my Virginia VA loans page for how the stack works.
