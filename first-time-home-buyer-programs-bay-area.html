CalHFA · California Housing Finance Agency

The State of California's First-Time Buyer Programs

Dream For All (Shared Appreciation)

California's headline program: substantial down payment help in exchange for sharing a portion of your home's future appreciation. It runs as a short annual application window for eligible first-generation buyers - and you must already be pre-approved with a participating lender before the window opens. This is exactly why we start planning months ahead.

MyHome Assistance Program

A deferred-payment junior loan covering a portion of your down payment and closing costs, layered on top of a CalHFA first mortgage. Unlike Dream For All, it's available year-round.

CalPLUS with ZIP (Zero Interest Program)

A CalHFA first mortgage (FHA or conventional) paired with ZIP - a zero-interest, deferred junior loan for closing costs. You repay it only when you sell, refinance, or pay off the loan.

GSFA · Golden State Finance Authority

Down Payment Help Without a Lottery

GSFA Platinum - Conventional or FHA

Down payment and closing cost assistance as a gift or forgivable second, paired with an FHA or conventional first mortgage. No application windows or lotteries - it's available whenever you're ready, with flexible credit requirements and no first-time-buyer restriction on some options.

Who it fits

Buyers with steady income but limited savings - including many teachers, first responders and healthcare workers who qualify for enhanced 'Select' features. Assistance amounts and terms vary with the rate option you choose, which is where I earn my keep: structuring the combination that nets you the most.

Straight from the source: GSFA Platinum program details · GSFA current programs & published rates

Federal Home Loan Bank · WISH Grant

Your Savings, Matched

WISH First-Time Homebuyer Grant

A savings match program - every dollar you've saved gets matched several times over, up to the current annual cap - for first-time buyers within income limits, through participating lenders, with homebuyer education required. Funding is annual and goes fast.

Smart Start - The Grant That Comes From Us

CCM Smart Start Grant

Unlike everything above, Smart Start is our own internal program - no outside agency, no waiting list, no lottery. If you're a first-time buyer under your county's income limit, qualifying happens right inside your pre-approval, and the grant goes toward your down payment. Questions

Straight Answers

Which first-time buyer program is best in the Bay Area?
There's no universal best - Dream For All gives the most help but has a narrow annual window and first-generation rules; GSFA Platinum is always available; MyHome and ZIP layer onto CalHFA loans; WISH multiplies savings; Smart Start comes straight from us. The best one is the best one you actually qualify for - which takes me about 15 minutes to determine.
Do I have to be a first-time buyer for all of these?
No - 'first-time' usually means no ownership in the last three years, and some GSFA options don't require it at all. Don't self-reject before we check.
Are the rates for these programs public?
Yes - CalHFA and GSFA both publish rates (linked above). Assistance programs sometimes price slightly differently than standard loans, which is part of the stacking math we do together.
How do I keep up with program changes?
You don't have to - the official links on this page always show current status, and when you work with me I check every program's funding the week we apply.
