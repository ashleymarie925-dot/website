The Options

Four Ways to Fund an ADU (and When Each Wins)

1. HELOC - fastest and most flexible if you have strong equity: draw as you build, pay interest only on what you use, keep your existing first mortgage untouched. Wins when your current rate is low and the project is mid-sized.

2. Cash-out refinance - one new loan, cash at closing, fixed payment. Wins when your existing rate is already high (you were refinancing anyway) or you want the certainty of fixed costs.

3. Renovation loans (HomeStyle / FHA 203k) - borrow against the home's future value including the finished ADU - powerful when today's equity isn't enough, and available on purchases too (buy the house AND fund the ADU in one loan).

4. Construction financing - for bigger builds; converts to permanent financing when done.

The kicker most owners don't know: on some loan types, projected rental income from the ADU can help you qualify - guidelines on this have modernized in recent years, and it changes the math dramatically for East Bay lots. Backyard cottage in Concord or a garage conversion in Brentwood, the numbers work more often than people think.

Questions

Straight Answers

Can I use the ADU's future rent to qualify for the loan?
On certain renovation and refinance programs, yes - a portion of documented market rent for the ADU can count toward qualifying income, subject to program rules. This is often the difference-maker, and it's exactly what I check first.
HELOC or cash-out refi - which is better for my ADU?
If your current mortgage rate is low, a HELOC usually protects it; if your rate is high, a cash-out refi may solve two problems at once. I'll run both against your actual balance and rate in minutes.
What if I don't have enough equity yet?
Renovation loans (HomeStyle, FHA 203k) lend against the after-completion value - the house plus the finished ADU - which is how buyers fund ADUs on purchase and newer owners build without waiting years for equity.
Are there grants for ADUs in California?
California has run ADU grant programs (for pre-development costs) that open and close with funding cycles. I monitor current availability - ask me when we talk.
