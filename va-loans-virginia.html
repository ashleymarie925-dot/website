The Benefit

Virginia Is VA-Loan Country

Virginia - especially Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake and Northern Virginia - is one of the busiest VA-loan markets in America, and for good reason: $0 down, no monthly mortgage insurance, no loan limit with full entitlement, and rates that regularly beat conventional. If you're PCSing in, buying near base, or finally putting down roots after service, this is the strongest tool in your toolbox.

I work PCS timelines for a living - pre-approval before you land, remote closings when you can't be there, and rental-conversion math if you're keeping your last house.

The Stack

The Combo Almost Nobody Explains

Here's the play most Virginia lenders never bring up: Virginia Housing (formerly VHDA) offers a Closing Cost Assistance Grant specifically for VA and USDA loans - meaning eligible buyers can pair their $0-down VA loan with a grant toward closing costs and bring remarkably little cash to the table. Income limits and first-time-buyer rules apply, and the details live on Virginia Housing's official site - always current. I'll tell you in one call whether you qualify for the stack.

Questions

Straight Answers

Can I combine a VA loan with Virginia Housing assistance?
Often, yes - Virginia Housing's Closing Cost Assistance Grant is designed for VA and USDA-RHS loans, subject to income limits and program rules. Stacked with $0 down, eligible buyers close with very little out of pocket.
I'm PCSing to Virginia - can I get pre-approved before I arrive?
Yes - we do the whole thing remotely, use your orders and LES for qualification, and time the closing to your report date.
Does Virginia's MCC tax credit still exist?
No - Virginia Housing suspended its Mortgage Credit Certificate program back in 2023, even though many websites still list it. The grant and second-mortgage programs are the active options; see my Virginia first-time buyer page for the current list.
Do you handle Hampton Roads and NoVa?
I'm licensed throughout Virginia - Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, Richmond, and Northern Virginia included.
