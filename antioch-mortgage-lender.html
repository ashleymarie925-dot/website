Questions

Straight Answers

What is the Antioch Home Ownership Program (AHOP)?
AHOP is the City of Antioch's down payment assistance program for first-time homebuyers, administered through BAAHA. It can provide significant down payment help for qualifying buyers who meet income limits and complete a HUD-approved homebuyer education course. Funding and amounts change, so text me and I'll check the current status for you.
Can I combine AHOP with CalHFA or other assistance?
Often, yes - stacking city, state, and lender programs is exactly how buyers with modest savings get into homes. The order and combination matter, which is why we plan it before you shop.
What credit score do I need to buy a house in Antioch?
FHA works with lower scores than most people expect, and conventional has its own thresholds. If you're not there yet, I'll build you a plan to get mortgage-ready - I do this with clients every week.
The house I want has leased solar panels. Is that a problem?
It doesn't have to be, but leases and PPAs have to be handled correctly in escrow and can affect your debt-to-income ratio. I deal with solar transfers on East County homes constantly - see my solar page or just ask.
