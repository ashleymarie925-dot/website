The Benefit

What a VA Loan Actually Gets You in California

Zero down payment - even at Bay Area prices. No monthly mortgage insurance - which is often the biggest monthly savings versus FHA or low-down conventional. And since 2020, veterans with full entitlement have no VA loan limit - the loan size is driven by your qualification, not a county cap. There's a one-time funding fee, and many disabled veterans are exempt from it entirely - something I check on every file because it's worth thousands.

The dirty secret in competitive California markets: some listing agents wrongly steer away from VA offers. My job is making your offer look bulletproof - fully underwritten pre-approval, appraisal strategy for the VA process, and me personally available to the listing agent to vouch for the file. VA buyers win with me because the lender does the extra work.

CalVet vs VA

CalVet Is Not the Same Thing

California also runs CalVet Home Loans - a separate state program with a different structure (it's technically a contract-of-sale arrangement, with its own rates, eligibility and insurance). Sometimes it wins, often the federal VA loan wins. Don't let anyone conflate them - we'll compare both against your situation before you commit to either.

Questions

Straight Answers

Is there really no loan limit on VA loans in California?
With full entitlement, yes - no VA-imposed limit since 2020. Your income, credit and residual income drive the approval instead. With partial entitlement (a prior VA loan still active), county limits can come into play - I'll calculate exactly where you stand.
Do VA loans have mortgage insurance?
No monthly mortgage insurance at all - a one-time funding fee applies instead, which can be financed, and many veterans with service-connected disability ratings are exempt from it.
Can I use a VA loan more than once?
Yes - entitlement can be restored after a sale, and second-tier entitlement can even allow two VA loans at once in some situations. This is exactly the kind of math I run for you.
Are VA offers really weaker in competitive markets?
They don't have to be. A fully underwritten pre-approval, a lender who answers the listing agent's call, and smart appraisal strategy make VA offers compete head-to-head - I've closed them in multiple-offer situations across the East Bay.
