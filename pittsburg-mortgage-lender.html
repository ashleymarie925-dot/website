Questions

Straight Answers

Are there first-time homebuyer programs in Pittsburg?
Pittsburg doesn't have an active city program right now, but Pittsburg buyers can use Contra Costa County and California programs - CalHFA loans, the WISH matching grant (a 4-to-1 savings match), CHDC's CalHome seconds, and lender grants like Smart Start. I'll check current funding for each.
Can I buy a house with an ITIN in Pittsburg?
Yes. ITIN loan programs exist for buyers without a Social Security number - larger down payments apply, but homeownership is absolutely possible. Se habla espanol on my team.
What credit score do I need for FHA?
FHA works with lower scores than most people expect - and sometimes lower still with more down. If you're close, I'll show you the fastest score moves - like paying specific cards below 30% - before we apply.
How fast can I get pre-approved?
Usually same day. We do the application by phone, run a soft credit pull that doesn't hurt your score, and you'll know your number before the weekend open houses.
