The Washington State Housing Finance Commission Programs

Home Advantage - WSHFC's flagship: a first mortgage paired with down payment assistance up to a few percent of your loan as a deferred, low/no-interest second - no payments until you sell, refinance or pay off. A needs-based option offers additional help for lower incomes.

House Key Opportunity + Opportunity DPA - a below-market first mortgage for buyers within income and price limits, pairable with deferred down payment assistance in the five figures.

Veterans DPA - additional deferred assistance specifically for Washington veterans and their families - and yes, it can complement a VA loan.

Covenant Homeownership Program - Washington's landmark program providing substantial down payment assistance (up to a large share of the purchase price, with caps) for buyers whose families were affected by discriminatory housing covenants before 1968, recently expanded to higher income limits with forgiveness features. As of August 2026 the program is active but under federal review, so terms may change - check WSHFC's official site for current status, and I verify eligibility at application time.

City programs - Seattle and Tacoma both run their own substantial down payment assistance for buyers within their limits, stackable with WSHFC firsts in many cases.

Straight Answers

What credit score do I need for WSHFC programs?
WSHFC programs are more forgiving than many expect, and homebuyer education (a WSHFC-sponsored class) is required - which is a feature, not a hurdle. I'll tell you exactly where you stand after one conversation.
Do I have to repay Washington down payment assistance?
Most WSHFC assistance is structured as a deferred second - no monthly payments, repaid when you sell, refinance, or pay off the home. Some newer options include forgiveness features. Terms vary by program and change; we confirm current terms at application.
What is the Covenant Homeownership Program?
A Washington program providing substantial down payment assistance to buyers whose families were harmed by racially restrictive covenants and related discrimination before 1968. It's active as of August 2026 but under federal review - eligibility is documented through a covenant verification process, and I check current status when you apply.
Can I use these with a VA loan?
In many cases yes - including WSHFC's veteran-specific assistance. See my VA loan pages, and we'll build the stack together.
