Questions

Straight Answers

Should I just use the builder's preferred lender in Oakley?
Compare. Builder incentives can be real, but they're often offset by rate or fees. I'll give you a same-day comparison so you can take the incentive with your eyes open - or beat it.
What is Mello-Roos and does Oakley have it?
Many newer Oakley neighborhoods carry Mello-Roos special taxes on top of regular property tax. It can add hundreds a month, so I include it in your affordability plan from day one.
How much down payment do I need in Oakley?
VA is $0 down, FHA is 3.5%, conventional starts at 3% - and down payment assistance can cover part of that. Many of my Oakley buyers bring far less cash than they expected.
Do you work with buyers who have solar leases to assume?
Yes, constantly - most East County resales have solar. The lease transfer has to be sequenced correctly with your loan. I quarterback that for you.
